Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 2026 Remaining (IST) ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information in connection with your use of our Chrome Extension and website.

1. Information We Collect

Our application is designed to prioritize your privacy. We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide the dashboard features.

A. Google Account Data

We use Google OAuth (Identity API) to authenticate you. This provides us with your email address and basic profile information to verify your identity within the extension.

B. Google Calendar Data

With your explicit permission, we access your Google Calendar data (read-only) to display your daily events and schedule on your personal dashboard. We do not store this data on any external servers. It is fetched directly from Google's API to your browser.

C. Google Drive Files

We may request access to create or edit specific configuration files in your Google Drive to sync your preferences or task lists across devices. We only access files created by this extension.

D. Local Storage

We store your preferences (such as widget layouts, theme settings) and local tasks (To-Do list items) in your browser's local storage or Chrome Sync storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the purpose of:

  • Providing and operating the 2026 Remaining (IST) dashboard.
  • Displaying your upcoming calendar events.
  • Syncing your preferences and tasks across your devices.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

3. Data Retention and Security

All personal data fetched (such as Calendar events) is processed locally on your device. We do not maintain a separate database of your personal information. Authentication tokens are stored securely in your browser's local storage and are used only to communicate directly with Google APIs.

4. Your Controls

You can revoke the extension's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: udaymehtani@gmail.com
